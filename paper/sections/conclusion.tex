\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
JET adapts pretrained language and vision-language models to typed decisions
through sampler-free candidate likelihood evaluation. Its execution design
combines shared prefixes, model-aware continuation scheduling, and preparation
overlap to reduce inference overhead while preserving outputs in the evaluated
comparisons. The results demonstrate practical local deployment on consumer
systems, including CPU-only execution, full GPU placement on an RTX 4090, and
mixed execution of a larger model on a desktop CPU and 16\,GiB Arc A770.

Optional thinking provides a further accuracy--throughput trade-off, with gains
that depend on the task and require empirical validation.

The measured 2.18--2.23-fold prefix-execution speedups and 30.8\% reduction in
RTX 4090 process time show that inference design materially affects the cost of
structured decisions without retraining model weights. Future evaluation should
establish matched comparisons with dedicated decision models, characterize
calibration and latency distributions, and quantify multimodal decision quality.
