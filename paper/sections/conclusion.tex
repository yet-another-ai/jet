\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
JET adapts pretrained language and vision-language models to finite decisions
through candidate likelihood evaluation without additional training.
The accuracy--throughput comparison in Figure~\ref{fig:mmlu-throughput} shows
the range of local deployment choices, from CPU-only execution to full and mixed
GPU placement. Qwen3.6-35B-A3B reaches 87.48\% on full MMLU and 3.69 requests/s
on a separately timed subset. Jev provides a reference for decision quality and
request rate, although differences in evaluation and timing prevent a controlled
performance ranking.

The 2.18--2.23-fold speedups from prefix reuse and cache management, and the
30.8\% reduction in RTX 4090 process time, show that inference optimizations
reduce cost while preserving outputs in the tested configurations. Optional
reasoning changes the accuracy--throughput trade-off and requires task-specific
validation. Future work should measure calibration, latency distributions, and
multimodal decision quality, and compare decision systems under matched conditions.
