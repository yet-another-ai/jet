\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
JET adapts pretrained language and vision-language models to finite decisions
through candidate likelihood evaluation without additional training.
The accuracy--throughput comparison in Figure~\ref{fig:mmlu-throughput} shows
the range of local deployment choices with full GPU placement on an Arc A770
and an RTX 4090. In the six-model full-MMLU Vulkan comparison on an RTX 4090,
Qwen3.5-27B has the highest accuracy, 85.09\% at 2.21 requests/s.
Qwen3.6-35B-A3B reaches 82.62\% at 4.17 requests/s, offering 1.89 times
the throughput at 2.47 percentage points lower accuracy.
Jev provides a reference for decision quality and
request rate, although differences in evaluation and timing prevent a controlled
performance ranking.

The 2.18--2.23-fold speedups from prefix reuse and cache management, and the
30.8\% reduction in RTX 4090 process time, show that inference optimizations
reduce cost while preserving outputs in the tested configurations. Future work
should measure calibration, latency distributions, and
multimodal decision quality, and compare decision systems under matched conditions.
