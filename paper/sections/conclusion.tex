\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
JET applies pretrained language and vision-language models to finite decisions
through mean-token candidate scoring. Prefix sharing and recurrent-state
isolation organize the required inference without changing this decision rule.
The RTX 4090 campaign measures six models with Vulkan and CUDA on full MMLU.
Its highest measured configuration is \jetresult{best}{model}, with
\jetresult{best}{accuracy}\% accuracy. Figure~\ref{fig:mmlu-throughput}
reports complete-process throughput from the same runs. Jev provides a
reference, although different evaluation and timing protocols prevent a
controlled performance ranking.

The LoRA-adapted Qwen3.5-4B and separately sourced Q8\_0 reference score
\jetresult{full-4b-lora-cuda}{accuracy}\% and
\jetresult{full-4b-cuda}{accuracy}\%, respectively, under the same CUDA
evaluation protocol. Their signed accuracy difference is
\jetresult{paired-lora}{gain} percentage points. Source and conversion
differences prevent a LoRA-only causal claim, and one timing run per variant
does not establish a repeatable speedup.

The RTX 4090 campaign is complete. Arc A770 measurements and a controlled
shared-prefix study remain pending. Further evaluation should
address calibration, latency distributions, multimodal decision quality,
and application-specific holdouts, with matched decision-system baselines.
