JET uses pretrained language and vision-language models to select among a
finite set of answers without requiring additional training. It scores each
candidate by its mean conditional token log-probability and normalizes these
scores across candidates. Shared-prefix execution reduces repeated inference
work while isolating candidate state. The RTX 4090 evaluation covers all
14,042 MMLU test questions with six models under both Vulkan and CUDA, plus
a LoRA-adapted Qwen3.5-4B under CUDA. The highest measured configuration,
\jetresult{best}{model}, has \jetresult{best}{accuracy}\% accuracy.
The adapted 4B model scores \jetresult{full-4b-lora-cuda}{accuracy}\%,
compared with \jetresult{full-4b-cuda}{accuracy}\% for a separately sourced
Q8\_0 reference. These comparisons characterize deployed artifacts rather
than isolate the effect of adaptation. Jev and published larger-model results
provide external context under different protocols. The four Arc A770
full-MMLU runs are complete; the shared-prefix comparison remains future work.
