JET adapts pretrained language and vision-language models to typed decision tasks
without additional training. It scores semantic candidates without token sampling,
using shared-prefix caching and architecture-aware candidate scheduling to reduce
redundant computation. Experiments demonstrate practical local deployment on
desktop CPUs and consumer GPUs, reaching 87.48\% accuracy on full MMLU at
3.69 requests per second. Execution optimizations provide up to 2.23-fold speedups
with identical responses. Optional thinking offers a task-dependent
accuracy--throughput trade-off.
